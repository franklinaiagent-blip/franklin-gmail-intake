Franklin Gmail Intake

Franklin Gmail Intake is a private, operator-managed tool for importing authorised Gmail messages and attachments into a local evidence and document-management workspace.

What it does

It preserves original messages and attachments, records their provenance, identifies exact duplicate files and routes relevant material for review or into the operator's designated evidence folders.

Read-only Gmail access

The application requests only Google's gmail.readonly permission. It does not send email, delete or archive messages, change labels or mark messages as read. Its configured sources are an intake Inbox and a separate account's designated label. Google's permission is mailbox-wide; the narrower source selection is enforced by the application.

Who can use it

This is not a public email service or a public sign-up portal. Google authorisation is managed separately by the operator for authorised accounts. This website provides information only and has no Gmail sign-in or mailbox access.
